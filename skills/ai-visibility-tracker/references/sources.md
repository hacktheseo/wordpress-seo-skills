# Which sources the engines cite, and what to do about each

## What the studies measured

Shares below use three different denominators (share of all citations, share
of the top sources, share of answers citing the domain). Never compare them
across studies, and always date them: ChatGPT's citations of Reddit and
Wikipedia collapsed in September 2025 in Semrush's data, and came back in
Ahrefs' 2026 data.

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| Ahrefs Brand Radar, US, updated 2 September 2026 | ChatGPT: Reddit 16.8 %, Wikipedia 7.0 %, YouTube 2.5 % of top source citations. AI Overviews: YouTube 22.9 %, Reddit 18.5 %, Facebook 10.1 %. AI Mode: Reddit 17.9 %, YouTube 17.8 % |
| Peec AI, March 2026, 30 million sources | Reddit ahead of YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia, Forbes overall; ChatGPT leans to Wikipedia and Forbes, Perplexity to Reddit, LinkedIn and G2, Google to Facebook and Yelp |
| Ahrefs, June 2025 | Only 7 of the top 50 sources are shared by ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews |
| Ahrefs, March 2026, 863 000 keywords | URLs cited by AI Overviews that rank in Google's top 10: 76 % in July 2025, 38 % in March 2026 |
| Ahrefs, 2025 to 2026, 15 000 long tail queries | 80 % of assistant citations do not rank in Google's top 100 for the query |
| BrightEdge, August 2025 | ChatGPT, AI Overviews and AI Mode disagree on the brands for 61.9 % of queries |

Two conclusions for a client: the engines do not agree with each other, so
report engine by engine; and ranking first on Google is less and less the
same thing as being cited, so the source gap has to be measured, not assumed.

## What to do with each kind of core source

| Kind | What it is | The action | The line not to cross |
|---|---|---|---|
| Media or blog | an article the engines use as evidence | pitch the author a correction, fresh data or a quote; offer an expert | paying for a mention without disclosure |
| Forum or video | Reddit, Quora, YouTube, LinkedIn threads | answer under the brand's own disclosed account, with facts; publish the video the thread lacks | fake users, bought upvotes, astroturfing: platforms ban it and engines learn it |
| Reviews or directory | Pages Jaunes, Trustpilot, G2, marketplaces | be listed with complete facts, ask real customers for reviews | fake or incentivised reviews, illegal in France and the EU |
| Competitor | the competitor's own page | publish a fair comparison page that answers the same question | disparaging claims |
| Encyclopedia | Wikipedia, Wikidata | check the brand's facts; propose corrections through the talk page | editing about your own company in breach of the conflict of interest rules |
| Public body | service-public.fr, legislation | cite them on your pages, align your figures with theirs | none, they cannot be influenced |

Rotating domains (back once or twice) are not worth a plan. Watch them in the
next wave.
